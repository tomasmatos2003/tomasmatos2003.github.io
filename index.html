About

I'm Tomás, a Portuguese MSc student in Cybersecurity at the University of Aveiro (DETI/IEETA). My work sits close to the metal — I like taking things apart to understand exactly how they behave.

The areas I care about most are reverse engineering, binary analysis & exploitation, applied cryptography, and Trusted Execution Environment (SGX) security. My master's thesis builds an Intel SGX–backed FIDO2 authenticator, so I spend a lot of time between enclave internals, cryptographic protocols, and low-level debugging.

Outside of academic work I'm a regular on CTFs, mostly on reversing and pwn, and I'm currently looking to grow into reverse engineering professionally.
