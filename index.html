About

I'm Tomás Matos. I'm a cybersecurity graduate from the University of Aveiro, Portugal, passionate about reverse engineering, malware analysis and application security.

The areas I care about most are reverse engineering, binary analysis & exploitation, applied cryptography, and Trusted Execution Environment (SGX) security. My master's thesis built an Intel SGX–backed FIDO2 authenticator, which meant a lot of time between enclave internals, cryptographic protocols, and low-level debugging.

Outside of academic work I'm used to participating on CTFs, mostly on cryptography, reversing and pwn, and I'm currently looking to grow professionally in reverse engineering and offensive security.

CTF

CTFs are where I sharpen the practical edge — mostly reverse engineering and pwn, occasionally crypto and forensics. A few pieces of work I'm happy with:

Magecart skimmer analysis

De-obfuscated a live web skimmer (base64 + rotation, AES-128-CBC exfil), documented IOCs and breach-notification implications for CSIRT reporting.

libc-2.31 ROP chain

Stack overflow exploited under IBT constraints — ret2plt puts@PLT leak with careful PLT-entry selection to satisfy endbr checks.

nanoGPT challenge

Reconstructed a byte-level language model, wrangled heteronym special tokens and beam-search decoding to recover the flag.

Controller-cipher (boroCTF)

Reverse-engineered a PlayStation controller button-encoding scheme into a decodable cipher.
